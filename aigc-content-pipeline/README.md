# AIGC Content Pipeline + Ad-Claims Checker

**Status:** coming soon

A pipeline that turns one product brief into listing copy, social posts and short-video scripts with AI. It includes a checker that flags risky advertising claims under North American rules (Competition Act, FTC, Health Canada, Amazon policies) and suggests safer wording. The checker will be tested on 20 sample ads with planted violations.

*This is a personal project. The checklist is not legal advice.*
